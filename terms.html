The fine print

Terms & Conditions.

Plain-language rules so every detail goes smoothly. By booking with JAM Detailing, you agree to these terms.

1. Pricing and quotes

  • Package prices on our website are starting prices for a coupe or sedan.
  • Larger vehicles add a flat amount: Small SUV +$10, Truck +$20, Large SUV +$25, MiniVan +$25.
  • The estimated total in the booking form is an estimate, not a final price.
  • Vehicles with heavy pet hair, sand, mud, stains, odors or other extra-heavy soiling may cost more. We'll always tell you the final price and get your OK before any work starts.

2. Payment

  • Nothing is charged online. Payment is made in person at your appointment, after we confirm the price and before cleaning starts.
  • We'll let you know which payment methods we accept when we confirm your booking. More payment options are coming soon.
  • Add-ons such as the Air Freshener and Truck Bed Wash are paid together with your package.

3. Booking, rescheduling and cancellations

  • Sending the booking form is a request, not a confirmed appointment. Your booking is confirmed once we reach out and agree on a day and time.
  • Need to change or cancel? Please call or text us at least 24 hours before your appointment.
  • If we can't reach you or access the vehicle within 15 minutes of the scheduled time, we may need to reschedule.
  • We may reschedule for bad weather (rain, dust storms or extreme heat) or other reasons outside our control. We'll contact you as early as we can.

4. What we need from you

  • Water access: we do not bring our own water. Please make sure we can use an outdoor water spigot close to where the vehicle is parked.
  • A safe place to work, such as a driveway, garage or parking spot where washing is allowed. If you live in an apartment or HOA community, please check that on-site car washing is permitted.
  • Please remove valuables and personal items before we arrive. We aren't responsible for items left in the vehicle.
  • Let us know about anything we should be careful with, like aftermarket parts, loose trim, or existing damage.

5. Service and results

  • We work carefully, but some stains, odors, scratches and wear can't be fully removed. We'll do our best and be honest about what to expect.
  • We aren't responsible for pre-existing damage, such as chips, scratches, faded or peeling paint, worn upholstery, or loose and broken parts.
  • If you aren't happy with something we did, tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back to make it right.

6. Contact
